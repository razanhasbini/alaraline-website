Usage

Directives

Do

  • Keep customer media edge-to-edge.
  • Crop decoration at the outer edges.
  • Use real waveform data and duration.
  • Keep the card compact and readable.
  • Let packaging provide most pink.

Don’t

  • Draw CSS circles as ornaments.
  • Use flat rectangles as ribbons.
  • Add generic holiday slogans.
  • Blur or tint over the product.
  • Turn the voice note into a chat bubble.
Responsive

Responsive composition

Desktop uses a 352px tool rail beside the Story. Tablet and mobile place a sticky 9:16 preview first and move every control below it. Export remains one click away.
