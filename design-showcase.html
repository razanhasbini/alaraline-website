09 · DO'S & DON'TS

Do

  • Use the real packaging and product photography for anything that shows a product.
  • Keep pink scarce; let silver and pearl do the seasonal work.
  • Keep every animation optional; the page is complete with motion off.
  • Offer a way to pause the snow.

Don't

  • No red-and-green, pine garlands, cartoon bows, candy canes or Santa.
  • Never redraw a product label or invent packaging that contradicts the photographs.
  • No snow, ornaments or sparkles over prices, names, labels or CTAs.
  • No glitter, rainbow foil or decorative gradients; gradients are only light.
