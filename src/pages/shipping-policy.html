Shipping policy

Thank you for shopping with ÀLara Line. We are dedicated to ensuring that your orders reach you quickly and safely, wherever you are. Our shipping policy outlines the process, delivery timelines, and important details to make your shopping experience smooth and worry-free.

Order Processing Time

  • All orders are typically processed within 2–5 business days after we receive your payment confirmation.
  • Orders placed on weekends or public holidays will be processed on the next business day.

Shipping Fees

Shipping rates are calculated according to the shipping method selected, the weight and dimensions of your items, and your delivery location.

  • The exact shipping cost for your order will be displayed during checkout, before you complete your purchase.
  • We partner with trusted carriers to ensure your order arrives safely and on time.

Delivery Timeframes

  • Delivery times depend on your location, the shipping method chosen, and any unforeseen circumstances (such as weather or carrier delays).
  • Once your order is confirmed, you will receive an email with an estimated delivery date, based on your shipping address and selected shipping option.

Worldwide Delivery

We proudly ship our products to many international destinations.

Customs duties, taxes, or import fees may apply depending on your country’s regulations. These charges are the customer’s responsibility.

Track Your Shipment

All shipped orders come with tracking details.

Once your package has been dispatched, you’ll receive an email containing your tracking number along with instructions to follow your delivery in real time.
